# Roadmap

What's planned after the 0.2.0 beta, roughly in priority order. Nothing here has a date; priorities depend on what testers report.

## Stability

- Reproduce and fix falling through the floor.
- Reach and test the Rollers level and the levels after Cheddar Gorge, using testers' reports and logs.

## Visual quality

- Fix the occasional shadow flicker, the rare FSR ghosting and the stray triangles in out-of-bounds water.
- Keep the HUD in a centred 16:9 area on ultrawide screens.

## Performance

- Reduce dips in heavy areas, which come from the game's chained SPU jobs, and the audio crackle that comes with them.

## Features

- Split-screen co-op.
- Online play through community servers (re-psn / Horizon).
- Possibly one launcher for all three Resistance ports.
